## Photo shoot (for YouTube + site)
Sunday action shots of Josh preaching come out mouth-open and odd. A 60-90 minute planned shoot fixes that and covers months of thumbnails.

Shot list:
- **Josh, thumbnail set:** 15-20 looks, chest up, plain or blurred background. Faces: serious, warm smile, curious/questioning, pointing, hand gesture, laughing, arms crossed, looking off to the side. Leave empty space on one side for title text.
- **Josh, staged "preaching":** on the platform, lights on, Bible in hand, mid-gesture but mouth closed. Shoot in burst and pick.
- **Josh + family / team:** for About.
- **Building:** outside in good light (morning or golden hour), entry, lobby, sanctuary empty and full.
- **People:** greeting at the door, coffee, kids check-in, worship hands up (wide, from behind), small groups at a table. Real smiles, not posed rows. Get photo consent for kids.

Tips for whoever shoots:
- Phone is fine if it's a newer one. Use portrait mode for Josh, landscape for everything else.
- Light from the front or side, never behind him. A window works.
- Shoot more than you think: 200 frames to keep 20.
- Send the originals (Google Drive or AirDrop), not texts. Texting crushes the quality.
